# Seva AI - Smart Volunteer Coordination

**AI-Powered Volunteer Coordination Platform for NGOs & Social Impact**

An intelligent system that digitizes paper surveys using **Google Gemini AI OCR**, visualizes urgent community needs on a live heatmap, smartly matches volunteers, and tracks real community impact.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Local social groups and NGOs collect important information about community needs through **paper surveys and field reports**. However, this valuable data is often scattered, making it hard to identify the biggest problems quickly and match volunteers effectively.

## ✅ Solution

**Seva AI** solves this by:
- Instantly digitizing paper surveys using **Google Gemini AI OCR**
- Showing live needs on an interactive **Bengaluru Heatmap**
- Providing smart volunteer recommendations
- Generating AI insights and tracking community impact

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📸 **Real Google Gemini AI OCR** – Upload paper survey images and get structured needs
- 🗺️ **Live Needs Heatmap** – Visual representation of urgent needs in Bengaluru
- 🙏 **Volunteer Portal** – Personalized "Seva Opportunities" with one-click acceptance
- 📊 **NGO Dashboard** – Real-time stats, priority analysis & allocation suggestions
- 📈 **Impact Dashboard** – Track volunteer hours, needs resolved, and people helped
- 🔄 **Auto Map Updates** – New needs appear instantly on the map

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JavaScript
- **AI**: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (for OCR)
- **Mapping**: Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap
- **Charts**: Chart.js
- **Deployment**: Ready for Vercel / Netlify
